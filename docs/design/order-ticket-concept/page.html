Order Ticket Concept

A clean, keyboard-first redesign that covers every variation the current ticket supports: Buy / Sell, Market / Limit, DMA / TWAP / VWAP / POV, every TIF rule, the order lifecycle, and the panel, dialog and compact layouts. Strategies are defined as data, so new algos add an entry rather than new UI. Try the live ticket below with the keyboard. Companion to the Order Ticket Review.

Fully working with the keyboard. Hold Alt (⌥) to see shortcuts on the ticket. Prices, volumes and fills are simulated.

    What changed, and why

    Each change answers an issue from the review or a pattern from the research.

    Side is unmistakable

    Sell / Buy are the quote

    The two sides are big buttons carrying their touch price: Sell on the bid, Buy on the ask, spread and mid between. The whole header tints with the side, and so does Send.

    Nothing moves

    A fixed core, execution below

    Instrument, side, quantity and price never change position. Strategy and its parameters live in their own section underneath, so picking VWAP adds below rather than pushing the core down.

    Scales to new algos

    Strategies are data

    A type-to-filter picker replaces the four buttons, and each strategy's fields, units, defaults, ranges and TIFs come from a definition. A new algo is a new entry.

    Keyboard first

    Short tab path, typed shorthand

    Tab visits instrument, quantity, price, strategy and its fields, then Send. Side and type are one keystroke from anywhere. The instrument field doubles as an order line.

    Size in any terms

    Quantity understands value and ADV

    Type shares, a USD value or a % of ADV; the hint always shows the result in shares, value and %ADV, plus the current position.

    Price relative to the quote

    Bid, ask, mid and ticks

    b+1 means one tick above the bid. The hint shows where the price sits against the touch. Market replaces the price field with an estimate instead of a disabled box.

    Filter and explain

    Only valid TIFs, with a reason

    The TIF chip lists only what this type, strategy and phase allow. If a change makes the TIF invalid, it switches to Day and says so.

    Algos you can read

    A schedule preview

    Each strategy says what it will do: slice rate and finish time for TWAP, the window for VWAP, expected duration for POV.

    Confirm when it matters

    Review only above thresholds

    Normal orders send in one keystroke. Above the account's notional limit or outside the collar, Send becomes Review and lists every parameter; ↵ confirms.
