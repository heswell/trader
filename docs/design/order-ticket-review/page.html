Anatomy of today's ticket

One form core (useOrderTicket) rendered top to bottom. Every element sits in a wrapping flex row, so the field order is fixed and algo fields push everything below them down.

2sides: Buy, Sell
2order types: Market, Limit (Stop reserved)
4strategies: DMA, TWAP, VWAP, POV
4TIFs: Day, IOC, FOK, GTC
32valid side × type × strategy × TIF combinations
3layouts: docked panel, dialog, compact strip
  1. Instrument picker Salt ComboBox over instruments; hidden when showInstrumentPicker=false (RIC shown as a label).
  2. Quote strip Bid and Ask are buttons that copy the price into the limit field (disabled for Market). Last, change, trading phase (when not continuous) and a stale indicator.
  3. Side and order type Two segmented toggles on one row. Side shows its colour as a 4px underline on the selected button and the 2px top border of the ticket. Keys B / S.
  4. Strategy A third segmented toggle: DMA, TWAP, VWAP, POV. Always visible.
  5. Algo parameters Inserted above quantity when the strategy is not DMA. Free-text inputs; which ones appear depends on the strategy.
  6. Quantity and price Steppers. Quantity steps by lot (− / +), price by tick, band-aware (▼ / ▲). Arrow keys nudge, Shift ×10. Quantity accepts 10k, 1.5m. Price is disabled with a "Market" placeholder for market orders.
  7. TIF and account Two dropdowns. Invalid TIFs stay listed but disabled. Last used account remembered in localStorage.
  8. Risk preview Notional in instrument currency with indicative USD. Advisory warnings: max order qty, max notional, ±10% collar, no price.
  9. Status and actions Inline status of the submitted order (pending, accepted, partial, filled, rejected), a connection-blocked reason, Reset and a side-coloured submit that echoes side, quantity and RIC. F9 or Ctrl+Enter submits.

Trading phase

The quote strip shows the phase when it is not CONTINUOUS: Pre open, Opening auction, Closing auction, Post close, Closed, Halted. validateOrder rejects orders on a halted instrument and when the market is closed, and accepts IOC / FOK only in continuous trading. None of this is reflected in the TIF list.

What the permutations show

Observations from laying every variant side by side. These are the starting points for the redesign.

High

Side is too quiet for what it does

Buy and Sell use the same toggle style as order type and strategy. The only colour cues are a 2px top border, a 4px underline and the submit button. Switching side barely changes the ticket.

High

Fields move when the strategy changes

Algo parameters are inserted above quantity and price, so the two fields traders use most jump down a row on TWAP / VWAP / POV and back on DMA.

High

Three stacked toggles of equal weight

Side, order type and strategy are three segmented controls with the same visual weight. Strategy has nothing to do with side, and sits apart from the parameters it controls.

Medium

Algo parameters are inconsistent and bare

TWAP and POV take a duration in minutes; VWAP takes an end time. No start time, no units on the inputs beyond the label, no time picker, and no preview of when the order will finish or how much it will trade per interval.

Medium

Disabled price field for Market

Market keeps a disabled price stepper with a "Market" placeholder. It holds space and reads like a field that failed to load.

Medium

Invalid TIFs are only discoverable in the dropdown

Algo orders allow Day and GTC only; Market disallows GTC. These are disabled options, so the reason is never stated, and a TIF chosen earlier can become invalid after a strategy change.

Medium

Quote strip is small and not side-aware

Bid / Ask are clickable but look like text. No spread or mid. A buyer usually cares about the ask and a seller about the bid; both get the same weight.

Medium

Quantity aids are hidden

10k / 1.5m parsing exists but nothing says so. No notional entry, no % of ADV, no position, which matter most for algos (POV at 10% of a 2m ADV is a different order from 10% of 20k).

Low

Inconsistent stepper glyphs

Quantity uses − / + and price uses ▼ / ▲. In a wide panel the buttons sit far from the value.

Low

Submit label leaves out price and strategy

"Buy 5000 AAL.L" echoes side, quantity and RIC, but not the limit or the algo. The quantity isn't grouped.

Low

The compact variant isn't compact

It carries the strategy row and algo fields, so a ladder or grid-row strip grows to two or three lines.

Low

Account is a peer of TIF

Account is usually set once per session or desk, yet it sits at the same level as per-order fields, and an empty account only fails on submit.

Common patterns, and where we stand

What nearly every ticket does, checked against ours.

Side is the loudest signal on the ticket

Buy blue or green, sell red, short a third colour. At least the submit takes the colour; often the whole ticket does (IBKR background, Sterling, TT buttons).

Partial: 2px border

Opened pre-filled from where you clicked

A bid, ask, depth level, position or chart price seeds price, quantity and often side. Bid vs ask can imply the side.

Have: prefill

Order type decides which fields exist

Market hides or disables price; stop-limit shows two labelled prices; algos reveal a parameter area.

Partial: disables price

Live bid / ask next to price entry

Often with sizes and a few levels of depth on hover; clicking a level sets the price.

Partial: small, no sizes

Tick-aware steppers, wheel and keys

Typing plus steppers, mouse wheel in pro tools, preset quantity buttons or number pads.

Have: keys, steppers

TIF and routing filtered by what's valid

Only list TIFs and options the instrument, route or algo accepts.

Partial: disabled, no reason

Relative price entry

Bid / Mid / Ask one-clicks, join / improve / take, or an offset in ticks or % from a base.

Partial: Bid / Ask only

Quantity in other terms

Notional or cash linked both ways with shares, % of position, % of balance or %ADV.

Missing

Position and shortability on the ticket

Current position near quantity; shortable / hard-to-borrow when selling more than held.

Missing

Algo parameters from definitions, with presets

Typed fields with defaults, required flags, ranges and hints; saved templates and one-click preset buttons.

Missing: hand-coded

Confirmation by threshold, with an opt-out

Confirm above a quantity, value, %ADV or price-away limit, showing every algo parameter. Otherwise one click or a hotkey.

Missing: no confirm

Stage / hold / save as well as send

Create the order without releasing it to market (IBKR Save, TT Hold and Stage, Eze staged tickets).

Missing

Double-send protection and post-send behaviour

Brief lock after submit, optional quantity reset, configurable focus after send.

Partial: spinner only

Retail and institutional

Pre-trade information

Retail shows money: buying power, fee, total, margin. Institutional shows impact and controls: %ADV, participation, value thresholds, price tolerance, restricted lists. Ours shows notional and account limits, closer to institutional.

Strategies

Retail folds algos into the order type list. Institutional separates broker or route from strategy, and strategies come with broker-defined parameters. Ours already treats strategy as separate from order type.

Customisation and safety

Pro tools let users rearrange the ticket, set tab order and presets, and rely on thresholds and hotkeys. Retail fixes the layout, hides extras under "More options" and adds friction such as slide-to-confirm.

Directions for the redesign

Starting points to discuss, combining the issues above with what the research shows works. None of these are decided.

Structure

Keep quantity and price fixed in place

Put instrument, side, quantity and price in a stable core that never moves. Place strategy and its parameters below the core in their own block, so switching DMA → VWAP adds below rather than pushing the core down.

Side

Make side unmistakable

Either two action buttons (TT style) or a toggle that tints the header and submit. Add a short-sell colour once short selling exists. Put the limit and strategy in the submit label: "Buy 5,000 AAL.L @ 435.7 · VWAP".

Algos

Describe strategies as data

One definition per strategy (fields, types, units, defaults, ranges, allowed TIFs) rendered by one form. Gives consistent start / end handling, presets, and a natural path to broker strategies later. Show a one-line schedule preview: "≈ 110 shares / min, finishes 15:30".

Quantity

Quantity, notional and %ADV as one control

Linked quantity ↔ notional, with %ADV and current position shown alongside. Expose the k / m shortcuts in a hint.

Price

A real quote block

Larger bid / ask with sizes and spread, the side-relevant touch emphasised, Mid as a third button, and an optional offset in ticks from a base (join, improve, take).

Rules

Filter and explain

Remove invalid TIFs rather than disabling them, say why in one line ("Algo orders run Day or GTC"), and quietly fix a TIF that becomes invalid after a strategy change.

Workflow

Threshold confirm and stage

Confirm only above notional, %ADV or collar thresholds, listing every algo parameter. Consider "Stage" next to Send for OMS workflows.

Compact

A true one-line strip

For ladders and grid rows: side buttons, qty, price, strategy as a dropdown with parameters in a popover, submit. No quote strip, no preview line.

Account

Account as context

Show the account in the ticket header as a chip, set once, with position for that account nearby. Flag an empty account before submit rather than on it.
