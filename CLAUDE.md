# trader

## Design boards

All UI design canvases are indexed in [docs/design/README.md](docs/design/README.md), grouped by area with a
status per design. Each has a folder under `docs/design/` with copies of its boards.

- Before any design or UI redesign work, read the index and the boards for that area, and stay consistent with them.
- When you create or change a design canvas, copy its `project/*` files into `docs/design/<slug>/`, and add or
  update its row and status in the index, in the same change.
